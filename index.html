What is an Akan Name?

Akan names are derived from the Akan people of Ghana and are based on the day of the week you were born. Each day corresponds to a specific name for males and females. For example, if you were born on a Monday, your Akan name would be "Kwadwo" for males and "Adwoa" for females. They are not merely labels but reflect the circumstances of a child’s birth and are believed to influence a person's soul and destiny.
